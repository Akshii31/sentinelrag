# Critical Vulnerability (CVE) Triage

## Initial assessment
Identify affected products and versions, the published advisory, severity information, whether the vulnerable component is enabled, and whether the system is exposed to the relevant attack path. A high severity score alone does not establish that a particular environment is exploitable.

## Mitigation and patching
Notify the system owner and security team. Follow change-management procedures to test and apply vendor-approved patches or mitigations. If an immediate patch is not available, assess vendor guidance and approved compensating controls. Do not make unreviewed production changes solely from an AI-generated recommendation.

## Evidence and follow-up
Record affected assets, versions, exposure, advisory links or identifiers, risk decision, mitigation owner, change approval, test result, and target completion date. Verify the patched version or mitigation and update the vulnerability tracker after the owner confirms completion.
