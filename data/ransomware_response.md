# Suspected Ransomware Response

## Immediate actions
Treat a credible ransomware alert as a high-priority incident. Contact the incident response lead and follow the approved containment playbook. If instructed by the authorised response team, isolate affected endpoints using approved controls. Avoid powering systems off or deleting files unless the response lead directs it, because evidence and recovery needs vary by incident.

## Protect evidence and backups
Preserve detection alerts, affected hostnames, timestamps, ransom-note samples where safe, and relevant endpoint/network logs. Do not connect backup media or clean systems to suspected infected endpoints without guidance. Do not attempt an unapproved decryption utility or negotiate with an attacker on behalf of the organisation.

## Escalation and recovery
Coordinate containment, legal/compliance notifications, communications, and recovery through authorised incident leadership. Restore from backups only after the recovery team validates backup integrity and the environment is considered safe. Document all approved actions and decisions.
