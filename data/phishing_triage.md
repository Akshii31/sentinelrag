# Phishing Email Triage

## Initial assessment
Treat unexpected messages asking for passwords, payment, urgent action, or opening attachments with caution. Do not click links, open unexpected attachments, reply with credentials, or forward the message to other people. Report the message using the organisation's approved phishing-reporting channel.

## Evidence to preserve
Preserve the original email, full headers when available, sender address, timestamp, displayed link text, actual link destination, and attachment filename. Do not upload potentially sensitive messages to public scanning services unless authorised by company policy.

## Escalation
Contact the IT security team or helpdesk. If credentials were entered, report that fact immediately so the authorised team can assess account protection steps. Do not claim a message is confirmed malicious based only on one indicator.
